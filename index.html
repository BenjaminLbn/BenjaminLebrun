About

I explore mind perception in robots.

I am a French neuropsychologist with a PhD in psychology. I study how people perceive, judge and interact with social robots and other artificial agents. My work draws on cognitive and social psychology and on my clinical background in neuropsychology, and I aim to bring these fields closer together in my research.
