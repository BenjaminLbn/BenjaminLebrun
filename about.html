About

I am a French neuropsychologist with a PhD in psychology. My research explores how people perceive phantom costs in human-robot interaction (HRI), including attributions of negative intentions and risks. I specialise in cognitive and social psychology with a background in neuropsycho‑cognitivo psychology. My career goal is to become a university professor.
